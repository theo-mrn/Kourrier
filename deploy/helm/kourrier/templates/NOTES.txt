Send an e-mail from inside the cluster:

  curl -X POST http://{{ include "kourrier.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc:{{ .Values.service.port }}/v1/emails \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer <api key>" \
    -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -H "Idempotency-Key: <unique id>" \
    -d '{"to":[{"email":"jane@example.com"}],"template":{"name":"welcome","locale":"fr","data":{"name":"Jane"}}}'
